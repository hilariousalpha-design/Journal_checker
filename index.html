What JournalCheck checks

🔎 Journal identity

Title, ISSN, eISSN, publisher, country and language from the master dataset.

📚 Indexing profile

SCIE, SSCI, AHCI, ESCI, JCR 2025, Scopus and Scopus Active fields.

📋 Publication data

Source type, open-access field, coverage and subject categories where available.

⚠️ Transparent screening

Missing information is shown as unavailable; it is never treated as proof of misconduct.

Methodology

Evidence first. Interpretation second.

JournalCheck is a screening tool built around the current master dataset. It separates positive, negative and unavailable fields and does not claim live verification against external indexing services.

1. IdentifySearch by title, ISSN or eISSN.
2. CompareReview the indexing and publication fields returned by the dataset.
3. ConfirmFor a submission decision, confirm critical requirements with the relevant official source.
