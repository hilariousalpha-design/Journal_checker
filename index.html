What JournalCheck will check

📚 Indexing

Check SCIE, SSCI, AHCI, ESCI, JCR and Scopus indexing against our datasets.

⚠️ Risk Screening

Identify publication-transparency and other warning indicators.

🤖 AI Explanation

AI will explain the collected evidence in a simple researcher-friendly report.

Methodology

Evidence first. AI second.

JournalCheck is designed as a screening and verification tool. Missing information will not automatically be treated as proof of misconduct. The system will distinguish verified evidence, unavailable evidence and warning indicators.
