WHAT JOURNALCHECK LOOKS AT

More than an indexing badge.

Separate the journal's recorded evidence from external confirmation and interpretation.

◈

Identity

Title, ISSN, eISSN, publisher, country and language, with identifier-first matching.

▦

Indexing profile

SCIE, SSCI, AHCI, ESCI, JCR, Scopus and Scopus Active fields from the master dataset.

◎

External confirmation

Where technically available, cross-check the ISSN with DOAJ, OpenAlex and Crossref.

△

Concern screening

A transparent Low / Moderate / High / Extreme concern profile based on evidence, not a misconduct verdict.
