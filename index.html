What JournalCheck checks

🔎 Journal identity

Title, ISSN, eISSN, publisher, country and language from the master dataset.

📚 Indexing profile

SCIE, SSCI, AHCI, ESCI, JCR 2025, Scopus and Scopus Active fields where available.

📋 Publication data

Scopus source type, open-access field and coverage information are shown without inventing missing values.

⚠️ Transparent screening

JournalCheck does not call a journal predatory merely because information is missing from this dataset.

Methodology

Evidence first. Interpretation second.

JournalCheck is a screening tool built around the current master dataset. It distinguishes positive fields, negative fields and unavailable information. It does not claim live verification against external indexing services.

1. IdentifySearch by title, ISSN or eISSN.
2. CompareReview the indexing and publication fields returned by the dataset.
3. ConfirmFor a submission decision, confirm critical requirements with the relevant official source.
